# Resistance Training: Evidence-Based Basics

## What is resistance training?

Resistance training uses external weights, machines, resistance bands, or body weight to challenge muscles.

## Core principles

### Consistency
Regular participation is more important than constantly searching for a perfect or complicated routine.

### Individualization
Training choices should reflect the person's goals, experience, available equipment, preferences, and physical limitations.

### Appropriate progression
Training should be adjusted over time according to the person's ability and response. Avoid assuming that one routine suits everyone.

### Exercise selection
Body-weight exercises, bands, machines, and free weights can all be useful options. A gym is not essential for every form of resistance training.

### Safety
Use suitable technique and manageable difficulty. Existing injuries, pain, or relevant medical conditions may require advice from a qualified professional.

## Important limitation

This article provides general education. It does not prescribe a personalized workout, specific loads, or a progression schedule.
