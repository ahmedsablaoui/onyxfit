# Physical Activity: Evidence-Based Basics

## What counts as physical activity?

Physical activity includes movement that uses energy, such as walking, cycling, sports, exercise, and everyday activities.

## Why it matters

Regular physical activity supports physical health, mental well-being, and overall function. Some activity is better than none.

## WHO recommendations

For adults aged 18–64:
- Aim for 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week, or 75–150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic activity, or an equivalent combination.
- Perform muscle-strengthening activities involving all major muscle groups on at least two days per week.

For children and adolescents aged 5–17:
- Aim for an average of at least 60 minutes per day of moderate-to-vigorous physical activity across the week.
- Include vigorous aerobic activity and muscle- and bone-strengthening activities at least three days per week.

These are general public-health recommendations, not individualized training prescriptions. Health conditions and individual circumstances may require adapted advice.
